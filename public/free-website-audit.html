Checklist

What the audit covers

Speed & Core Web Vitals

Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint and layout shift on mobile, with the specific files and scripts causing delays.

Technical SEO

Indexing, canonicals, redirects, sitemap, robots.txt, broken links and title and description issues.

Structured data

Which schema types you have, which are missing, and errors that block rich results.

AI search readiness

Whether AI crawlers can read your content, plus quick wins for appearing in Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT.

Accessibility basics

Contrast, alt text, headings and keyboard navigation problems that also affect SEO.

Conversion tracking

Whether GA4 records your enquiries, calls and sales correctly.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the audit really free?

Yes. You get a short, prioritised list of issues and fixes for your site. There's no obligation to hire me to fix them.

What do you need from me?

Just your website URL and what you want the site to achieve, for example more enquiries, more sales or better rankings. No logins or access are needed.

Which platforms can you audit?

Any public website: WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Next.js, Nuxt, or custom-built.
