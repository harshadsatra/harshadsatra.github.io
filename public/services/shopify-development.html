What I build

Harshad Satra is a freelance Shopify developer in Mumbai, India, who builds custom Online Store 2.0 themes for D2C brands. Recent stores include Pulp Society and First Edge Sports (2025), RucFit (2024), a weighted-backpack fitness brand, and Hyperice India (2022). Instead of tweaking a marketplace theme, he builds sections and blocks around your brand, so your team can rearrange pages in the Shopify editor while the store stays fast and on-brand.

He holds a certification in Shopify Theme Development (Online Store 2.0 + Tailwind CSS) and uses Liquid, Tailwind and a Gulp/SCSS build pipeline to keep themes lean. Every store launches with product schema, clean collection URLs, compressed images and a checked mobile checkout, because most D2C traffic in India comes from phones.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Should I use Shopify or WooCommerce?

Shopify suits brands that want hosted, low-maintenance selling with a polished checkout. WooCommerce suits stores that need deep customisation, content-heavy pages or no monthly platform fees. I build on both and recommend one based on your catalogue, budget and team; see WooCommerce development.

Can you customise my existing Shopify theme instead of building a new one?

Yes. If your current theme is close to what you need, I add custom sections, fix layout issues and speed problems, and remove unused apps that slow the store down.

Do you build Shopify stores for brands outside India?

Yes. Shopify works the same worldwide, and I work remotely with international clients. See working with international clients.

Will my team be able to edit the store after launch?

Yes. Every page is built from Online Store 2.0 sections and blocks, so you can add banners, reorder content and launch campaigns in the Shopify editor without a developer.
