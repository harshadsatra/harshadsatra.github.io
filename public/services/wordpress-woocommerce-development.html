WordPress & WooCommerce

What I build

Harshad Satra is a freelance WordPress and WooCommerce developer based in Mumbai, India. Since 2013 he has built nearly 30 WordPress websites listed in his portfolio, for corporate firms, architects, NGOs, food brands, real-estate developers and online stores. Every build uses a custom theme rather than a page-builder template, so the site loads fast, matches the design exactly and stays easy for your team to edit.

For online stores he builds on WooCommerce with custom product pages, checkout and payment flows, including Razorpay and Stripe, plus the plugins your business needs. For content-heavy sites he sets up custom post types and Advanced Custom Fields, so editors update content without touching code. Sites are hosted on managed WordPress hosting or AWS Lightsail, with backups, security hardening and technical SEO configured before launch.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why choose a custom WordPress theme over a page builder like Elementor?

A custom theme ships only the code your design needs, so pages load faster and score better in Core Web Vitals. It also can't break when a page-builder update changes its layout engine, and your editors get fields designed around your content rather than a generic drag-and-drop canvas.

Can you fix or speed up my existing WordPress site?

Yes. I start with an audit of plugins, theme code, hosting and Core Web Vitals, then fix the issues with the biggest impact first: removing unused plugins, optimising images and queries, adding caching and cleaning up the theme. See website speed & SEO optimisation.

Which payment gateways can you integrate with WooCommerce?

I have integrated Razorpay for Indian stores and Stripe for international ones, and WooCommerce supports PayPal and most other major gateways through official extensions.

Can you migrate my site to WordPress from another platform?

Yes. I move pages, posts, products and media, keep your URL structure or set up 301 redirects so you don't lose search rankings, and test everything on a staging site before switching over.
