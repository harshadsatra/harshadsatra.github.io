FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Nuxt.js good for SEO?

Yes. Nuxt renders pages on the server or at build time, so search engines and AI crawlers receive complete HTML instead of an empty JavaScript shell. Combined with Nuxt's meta and sitemap tooling, it performs as well as a traditional website for SEO.

Why use Directus as a headless CMS?

Directus sits on top of a normal SQL database, gives editors a clean admin panel, and exposes your content through REST and GraphQL APIs. You own the data, can self-host it, and aren't locked into one front-end framework.

Vue.js or React: which should I choose?

Both are excellent. Vue is often faster to build with for small teams and has a gentler learning curve; React has the larger ecosystem. If your team already uses one, stick with it. I build with both; see React & Next.js development.

Can you join my existing Vue project?

Yes. I regularly pick up existing Vue 2 and Vue 3 codebases, review the architecture, and take on features, refactors or a Vue 2 to Vue 3 migration.
