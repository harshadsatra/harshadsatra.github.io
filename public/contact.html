FAQ

Hiring a freelance web developer

What services does Harshad Satra offer as a freelance web developer?

I build custom websites and web apps with Vue.js, Nuxt.js, React and Next.js; WordPress, WooCommerce and Shopify stores; SaaS front ends; and AI-powered features such as chatbots and smart search. I also audit and fix existing sites for speed, SEO and accessibility, and handle deployment on AWS, Vercel, DigitalOcean or Netlify.

Do you work with clients outside Mumbai and India?

Yes. I work remotely with clients across India and abroad and have delivered projects for brands outside India, such as The Common Table in Europe. Calls and updates are scheduled around your time zone.

Can you make my existing website faster and rank better on Google?

Yes. Performance and technical SEO are part of every build I do. At DocuX I improved application load times by 30%. For an existing site I start with an audit of Core Web Vitals, crawlability and structured data, then fix the issues with the biggest impact first.

Which technologies and platforms do you use?

Vue.js, Nuxt.js, React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js and GSAP for animation, with WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify or Directus for content. I deploy to AWS, Vercel, DigitalOcean and Netlify. I recommend a stack based on your goals, budget and who will maintain the site.

How much experience do you have?

I have 13 years of web development experience, starting in 2013. I have worked with Leah, DocuX, Croma and Yellow Slice, led front-end teams, and delivered 28+ client projects listed in my portfolio.

How do we start a project?

Send a message through the form above or email hello@harshadsatra.in with your goals, timeline and budget range. I'll reply with questions or a proposal covering scope, cost and timeline.
