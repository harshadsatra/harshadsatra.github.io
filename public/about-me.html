Hey there!

My name is Harshad Satra. Approaching nearly a decade in web development, I'm a passionate architect of visually captivating and seamlessly functional websites. My mission extends beyond aesthetics; I'm dedicated to crafting web experiences that are SEO-friendly, fully accessible, and mobile-responsive.
