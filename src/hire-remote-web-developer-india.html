FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why hire a freelance web developer from India?

You get senior-level experience at a lower cost than a local agency, and the time difference can work in your favour: work progresses overnight and is ready for review in your morning. The key is choosing someone with a track record of international projects and clear communication.

How do we handle time zones?

I'm on IST (UTC+5:30). That gives a natural overlap with UK and European mornings and afternoons. For US clients I schedule calls in my evening (your morning), and most day-to-day communication happens asynchronously by email or on a shared task board.

How are payments and contracts handled?

The proposal sets out scope, milestones and the payment schedule in your currency. Work is invoiced per milestone, and the code and content are yours once the project is paid for.

Can you work with my in-house team or agency?

Yes. I regularly work alongside designers, marketers and other developers, using your repository, task board and review process.
