About these projects

This portfolio lists 28 client projects built by Harshad Satra, a freelance web developer in Mumbai, India. They range from brand and portfolio websites to e-commerce stores, real-estate sites, film and media platforms, and SaaS microsites, for clients in India and abroad. Most use one of three stacks: Nuxt.js or Next.js with a headless CMS such as Directus, custom WordPress and WooCommerce builds, or PHP and CodeIgniter for data-driven sites. Each project is responsive, SEO-ready and hosted on AWS, Vercel or a managed host chosen for the client's budget.

Highlights

  • Bridge Postworks: custom Nuxt.js site with Directus CMS, AWS deployment and a media streaming player.
  • InOrdinary: full WordPress and WooCommerce build with a custom payment flow, artist CMS and AWS Lightsail hosting.
